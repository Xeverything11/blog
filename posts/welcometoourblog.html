Welcome to our blog!

Created on November 18, 2026

Exactly 4 years ago, I created my Wikimedia account, and during 2022 to 2024 (and still today to some extent), I have contributed to some Wikimedia wikis, such as Wikibooks, Wikisource and Wikimedia Commons. As of the time of writing, my Wikimedia account has over 27,000 edits (27,094 to be exact). Since 2025, I have been contributing to wikis outside Wikimedia, such as wikiHow.

To celebrate my 4th anniversary, I have created a blog which I am posting stuff there. By the way, this underlying framework for this blog is Codexify, which I have been developing. Currently, the version is currently 0.10.0 at the time of writing, and I plan to release version 1.0 soon.
